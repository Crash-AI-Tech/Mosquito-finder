# Privacy Policy — Mosquito Finder (猎蚊者)

**Last updated: 2026-05-26**

---

## English

Mosquito Finder ("the App") is designed with your privacy as a top priority.

### Data Collection

The App **does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data**. All image processing and analysis occurs entirely on your device and is never sent anywhere.

### Camera

The App uses your device camera solely to perform real-time mosquito detection. Camera frames are processed locally in memory and are **never saved, uploaded, or shared**.

### Motion Sensors

The App reads device accelerometer and gyroscope data to detect approaching movements. This data is **processed locally in real time and never stored or shared**.

### App Settings

The App stores only your language preference (English / Chinese) locally on your device using standard iOS storage (NSUserDefaults). No personal information is involved.

### Third-Party Services

The App contains **no third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising, or tracking frameworks**.

### Network

The App makes **no network connections** of any kind. It runs fully offline.

### Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a revised date.

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact:  
**[your-email@example.com]**
