Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: 2026-05-26

English

Mosquito Finder ("the App") is designed with your privacy as a top priority.

Summary

We collect nothing. The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data of any kind. All image processing and AI inference runs entirely on your device.

Camera Access

The App uses your device's rear camera solely to perform real-time mosquito detection. Camera frames are processed in memory and are never saved to disk, never uploaded, and never shared with any third party.

Motion Sensor Access

The App reads accelerometer and gyroscope data to detect approaching movements and phone shakes. This sensor data is processed locally in real time and never stored or transmitted.

App Settings

The App stores only your language preference (English / Chinese) locally on your device using standard iOS storage (NSUserDefaults). No personal information is involved.

Third-Party Services

The App contains no third-party SDKs, analytics frameworks, advertising networks, or tracking libraries of any kind.

Network Connections

The App makes zero network connections. It runs fully offline. You can verify this with any network monitoring tool.

Data Retention

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to retain or delete.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. It complies with COPPA and equivalent regulations.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is ever updated, the new version will be posted at this URL with a revised date at the top.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please contact:

中文 · Chinese (Simplified)

猎蚊者（以下简称"本应用"）将您的隐私保护视为首要任务。

摘要

我们不收集任何数据。本应用不收集、不存储、不传输、不共享任何形式的个人数据。所有图像处理与 AI 推理均在您的设备本地完成。

摄像头权限

本应用仅使用设备后置摄像头进行实时蚊子检测。摄像头画面在内存中实时处理，绝不保存到磁盘、绝不上传、绝不向任何第三方共享。

运动传感器权限

本应用读取加速度计和陀螺仪数据，用于检测靠近动作和手机晃动。该传感器数据仅实时处理，不存储，不上传。

应用设置

本应用仅在您的设备本地存储语言偏好（中文 / 英文），使用 iOS 标准本地存储（NSUserDefaults），不涉及任何个人信息。

第三方服务

本应用不包含任何第三方 SDK、数据分析框架、广告网络或追踪库。

网络连接

本应用不进行任何网络连接，完全离线运行。您可使用任意网络监控工具进行验证。

数据保留

由于不收集任何数据，因此不存在需要保留或删除的内容。

儿童隐私

本应用不向包括 13 岁以下儿童在内的任何人收集数据，符合 COPPA 及同等法规要求。
