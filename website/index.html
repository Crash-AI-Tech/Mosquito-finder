How It Works

Three steps to find it

Designed to work in real-world low-light indoor conditions.

01

Open & Start Hunting

Tap Start Hunting. The flashlight turns on and the camera enters scan mode with the night-vision overlay.

02

Sweep Walls & Ceilings

Slowly move your phone across surfaces. The Stage 1 AI marks suspicious dark spots with yellow halos — walls, ceilings, curtains all work.

03

Zoom In to Confirm

Center a halo in the crosshair and move closer or zoom to 1.5×+. Stage 2 CoreML runs a classification — red halo + vibration means mosquito confirmed.
